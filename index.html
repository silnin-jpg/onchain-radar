Safety checklist

Use this before researching any token further.

  • Verify the mint / contract address independently.
  • Check whether liquidity is locked or can be removed.
  • Inspect holder concentration and deployer wallet history.
  • Check whether sells work; beware honeypots and transfer taxes.
  • Never trust paid boosts, social hype or a high scanner score alone.
